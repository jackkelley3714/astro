Caleb Williams and Drake Maye went first and third in the 2024 NFL Draft, so every meeting between them comes with a built-in question: who got the better franchise quarterback? On Thursday night at Soldier Field, the Chicago Bears gave their answer for one evening, beating the New England Patriots 30-24 in Week 7.

## How the game played out

Chicago came out fast. Cairo Santos opened the scoring with a 43-yard field goal, and D'Andre Swift punched in a 4-yard touchdown to make it 10-0 after the first quarter.

New England answered in the second quarter. Maye scored on a 7-yard run after a replay review overturned the original call, and the Bears still led 13-7 after another Santos field goal. Then came the sequence that shaped the half: Williams hit Luther Burden III for a 24-yard touchdown with 21 seconds left, only for Maye to fire back with a 48-yard touchdown pass to Stevenson with three seconds on the clock. Chicago went into the break up 20-14.

Williams found tight end Colston Loveland for a 12-yard score early in the third quarter, and Maye responded with a 30-yard touchdown pass to Raridon to make it 27-21. A 58-yard Santos field goal with 1:39 left pushed the lead to 30-21. New England added a field goal with 42 seconds remaining, but its onside kick attempt failed and the Bears closed it out.

## Williams by the numbers

Williams finished 22 of 33 for 308 yards, two touchdowns, no interceptions and a 116.7 passer rating. Chicago finished with no turnovers and 405 total yards, and Williams took three sacks without it derailing the offense.

Just over half of his yards, 161 of 308, came after the catch, which says as much about the Bears' receivers and the quick-game design as it does about the quarterback. Even so, the touchdown throw to Burden just before halftime was the kind of two-minute execution that Bears fans spent two seasons waiting to see.

## Maye kept it close

This was not a case of one quarterback overwhelming the other. Maye's line was just as strong on paper: 24 of 31 for 321 yards, two touchdown passes and a rushing touchdown. He also threw the game's only interception, and New England needed a trailing offense to do too much in the final minutes. The difference was turnovers, a long field goal, and a failed onside kick, not talent.

## Has the 2024 No. 1 pick finally delivered?

That is the question dominating fan conversation online, including on Reddit, and it deserves a careful answer. Williams returned last week from a hamstring injury that cost him three games and looked sharp, going 30 of 36 for 305 yards in a win over Atlanta. Thursday added a second strong outing against a good opponent, with the protection and ball security that were missing in his rookie year.

The honest read is that the trend is clearly positive, but two good games do not settle a debate. Both quarterbacks are still young, both are producing, and their careers will be compared for a decade. Thursday night went to Williams.

## What's next

The Bears improve to 5-2, while the Patriots fall to 4-3. Chicago's next game is on Monday Night Football at Seattle on Nov. 2.
